# Project Demonstration Videos Directory

Place your high-resolution MP4 or WebM project walkthrough recordings in this folder:

1. `ai_governance_demo.mp4` - Video walkthrough of AI Decision Governance Copilot
2. `jobpilot_demo.mp4` - Video walkthrough of JobPilot Multi-Agent DAG

### Alternative: Cloudflare, Cloudinary, YouTube or Loom
You can also host your videos on Cloudinary, YouTube (Unlisted), Loom, or Supabase Storage and paste the direct or embed URL directly into `src/services/knowledgeBase.js` in the `PROJECT_VIDEOS` array.

The video modal player automatically detects whether you are using a direct local video file, a cloud CDN stream, or a YouTube/Loom embed!
